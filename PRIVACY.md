# Copy for LLM Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Copy for LLM is developed by Selçuk Bilmiş. It prepares text from files and folders you select on your Mac.

## Processing and collection

File reading, PDF and Office document extraction, image text recognition (OCR), filtering, and token estimation run locally on your Mac. The app does not upload your files, extracted text, clipboard contents, or settings to the developer or an AI service. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or app account requirement.

## Local storage

The app saves settings and presets locally. It also saves the most recently copied output so you can use Re-copy Last Output; the next successful copy replaces it. A Finder Options selection is passed to the app using a local file containing paths and file-access bookmarks. This handoff is deleted when the app reads it, and selections older than two minutes are not used.

The Mac App Store edition stores shared data in its macOS App Group container. The direct-download edition stores it in ~/Library/Application Support/CopyForLLM. These files remain on your Mac until overwritten or removed; system backups may retain copies. You can remove local app data after quitting the app by deleting its CopyForLLM data folder. The app does not maintain a server-side copy.

## Clipboard and other services

When you copy output, it is placed on the macOS clipboard. macOS features such as Universal Clipboard and other apps with clipboard access may handle that content according to your system settings. If you paste it into an AI assistant or another app, that service's privacy policy applies. Review the preview before sharing sensitive material; filename filters cannot guarantee removal of every secret.

Optional notifications are managed by macOS and can be disabled in System Settings. Apple may process App Store and diagnostic information under its own privacy policies and your system settings. Opening support or privacy links visits GitHub in your browser and is subject to GitHub's privacy policy.

## Your choices and contact

You choose which files to process and what output to copy. To stop access, stop using the app or disable its Finder Quick Actions in System Settings. For privacy questions, contact the developer through https://github.com/sbilmis/copy-for-llm/issues. Issues are public; do not post private files, credentials, or personal information there.

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
